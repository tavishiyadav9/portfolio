## Subtitle

Market entry strategy for Perplexity's agentic AI browser in education, against Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI.

## Problem

Perplexity is entering education with Comet — an agentic AI browser — against Google (Gemini for Education), Microsoft (Copilot), and OpenAI. The brief: define a new market category, articulate Comet's differentiation, and design a GTM and monetization plan that can scale across classrooms, research labs, and enterprise campus systems.

## Role

Member of Team 238. Contributed to market sizing, competitive positioning, monetization design, and the 20+ page research report.

## Approach

Named and defined a new category — "Agentic Academic Intelligence" — to reframe the conversation away from chatbot comparisons toward proactive, LMS-integrated systems. Grounded Comet's differentiation in four pillars: contextual academic reasoning (Wiley scholarly integration), learning-ecosystem embeddedness (Canvas LTI, 47% NA market share), academic-integrity architecture (automatic citation, audit trails), and trust-first governance (FERPA, faculty toggling). Designed a three-phase GTM — grassroots student freemium → faculty and department LTI pilots → institutional enterprise licensing — with tiered monetization from Student+ ($5–10/mo) to Faculty Suite ($5–15K/yr) to Campus Enterprise ($50–500K/yr), plus a perpetual free tier for digital equity.

## Outcome

Delivered a 20+ page research report sized against the $1.72B → $8.46B (37.92% CAGR) agentic-AI-in-education market, with a three-year roadmap targeting 10 pilots → 50 institutions → 100+ institutions and 2M+ students, plus quantified use cases across student, faculty, and provost personas. Full submission included slide deck, research report, and screen-recorded demo of the team's Perplexity workflow.

## Stack

Market sizing, competitive analysis, GTM strategy, product positioning, monetization design, Perplexity Labs research
