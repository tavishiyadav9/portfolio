## Subtitle

Gamifying digital adoption for Hilton's luxury-brand guests, built on real guest survey and usage data.

## Problem

Hilton's luxury and full-service brands lag in digital tool adoption — digital check-in, digital key, and digital checkout all sit below 50% usage at luxury properties — even though full- and focused-service account for roughly 45% of quarterly revenue. Hilton needed a way to drive digital engagement without diluting the premium in-person experience.

## Role

Member of Team NextGen Hosts. Analyzed real Hilton guest survey, messaging, and digital-capability-usage data through Qualtrics. Contributed to opportunity framing, the three-layer solution design, risk mitigations, and the final pitch.

## Approach

Designed The Hilton Quest — a gamification layer inside the Hilton Honors app, built on three stacked components. An integration layer wires into Hilton Honors, the Hilton App, and digital messaging. A gamified layer turns each stage of the stay (pre-arrival through post-stay) into quests with XP, badges, and rewards. An AI personalization layer — Hilton Halo — uses reinforcement learning to adapt quest difficulty, timing, and reward value to individual guest profiles. Phased the rollout to mitigate risk: pilot in luxury Hilton-managed properties where data quality is highest, cap active quests per guest at 3–5 to avoid cognitive overload, and build in an opt-in privacy flow.

## Outcome

Delivered the full strategic pitch over 48 hours — opportunity sizing (&lt;20% digital adoption, ~45% revenue exposure), three-layer solution framework, risk mitigations (amenity data gaps, guest overload, staff alignment, privacy), and a go-to-market sequence (pilot → marketing launch → TikTok influencer partnerships → iterate on quest interaction data).

## Stack

Qualtrics (guest survey + messaging data), Hilton customer dataset, customer journey mapping, gamification design, competitive and market research
