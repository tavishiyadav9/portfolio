## Subtitle

AI Technical Lead on an agentic AI system built for Oracle through Cornell's Digital Technology Immersion program.

## Problem

Oracle wanted to explore how agentic AI — systems that plan and execute multi-step tasks rather than just answer questions — could be applied to enterprise healthcare-adjacent workflows, and what the architectural building blocks would need to look like in practice.

## Role

AI Technical Lead on a cross-functional Cornell Johnson DTI team, working directly with a Senior Principal Engineer on Oracle's Agentic AI team as technical mentor throughout the semester.

## Approach

Designing the agentic architecture end-to-end — agent building blocks, tool specifications, and the knowledge-base structure that grounds the agent's responses. Translating product requirements from the Johnson side into technical decisions, and working through OCI Generative AI as the deployment surface.
